GunWeight
---
- This is a simple `GTA:SA` mod that makes CJ slower dynamically based on how many weapons he has and how much ammo he has.
- All ammo and weapon masses are configurable via config.ini and hot-reload is supported with the cheat that is also found in `config.ini.`
- Error handling is not implemented for `config.ini` and therefore the game will most probably crash if you have a bad config!

CREDITS
---
- Gemini was used to find bugs and discover potential fixes for them, for discovering plugin-sdk methods and their usages and also **to get the default masses for weapons and ammo.**
- This mod also utilizes [metayeti's mINI library](https://github.com/metayeti/mINI) for parsing values from `config.ini`
- Distributed as FOSS under the `MIT license`.

Presented by: `vecondite`.

On a side note, I do not know how suitable this is for regular gameplay. Having heavy weapons (such as miniguns/flamethrowers) and large amounts of ammo can make CJ painfully slow
